How
we
work

No factories, no reprints. Just the steps between an idea and a shirt.

  1. 01

    Concept

    Every motif starts as a drawing, printed out and pinned to the mat before a single cut.

  2. 02

    Carve

    The drawing is traced onto board and cut by hand. Every line you see is a line carved away.

  3. 03

    Print

    Ink is rolled onto the block and pressed into the shirt by hand, one at a time.

  4. 04

    One run

    A design prints in a single small batch. No two come off the block quite the same.

  5. 05

    Gone for good

    Once a run sells out, the block is retired. What's left is what's left.
